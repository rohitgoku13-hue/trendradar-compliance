Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Trend Radar, an independent consumer trend and creator-intelligence product operated by its individual developer, uses and protects data.

About Trend Radar

Trend Radar helps people explore emerging public-video topics, content niches, supporting videos, and smaller creators showing unusual traction. The current public product uses public YouTube metadata. It does not access private YouTube account data or request authorization to a user's private YouTube account, and this flow uses no end-user Google or YouTube sign-in or OAuth grant.

Information Trend Radar Uses

Depending on availability from YouTube API Services, Trend Radar may use public video and channel identifiers, titles, publication timestamps, view counts, public subscriber-count observations, category information, public channel metadata, and associations between public uploads and channels.

Subscriber observations are public aggregate channel counts where available. Trend Radar does not receive a channel's private subscriber list or other private subscriber information.

YouTube API Services

Trend Radar uses the following YouTube Data API v3 methods for distinct, limited purposes:

  • search.list discovers emerging public videos and previously unknown creators through bounded searches.
  • videos.list retrieves current public video metadata and statistics needed to validate discovered videos.
  • channels.list verifies current public channel facts when those facts are required.
  • playlistItems.list checks recent public uploads from already-known qualifying creators so they do not need to be repeatedly rediscovered through Search.

Use of YouTube API Services is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's policies.

Purpose of Processing

Trend Radar processes this information to identify factual public-video signals, organize evidence into explorable categories, topics, and content niches, evaluate repeated creator performance, keep previously discovered creator evidence current, and present informational trend and creator intelligence. Derived analyses do not guarantee future performance.

Creator Discovery

For new creator discovery, Trend Radar uses bounded public-video searches, deduplicates discovered videos, retrieves current video facts, checks current channel facts where required, and performs a bounded review of eligible public upload history. Discovery continues over time because new public creators and videos appear.

Monitoring of Previously Discovered Creators

When a creator has already met the product's factual qualification rules, Trend Radar may monitor that creator's recent public uploads directly. This uses the creator's public uploads association, followed by current video facts and a channel refresh only when needed. Direct monitoring reduces redundant discovery searches. A creator with insufficient current signal may be reconsidered later, and an out-of-range creator may receive less frequent processing until scheduled revalidation.

Data Minimization

Trend Radar requests and processes only the public fields needed for its defined discovery, monitoring, validation, and presentation purposes. It limits searches, deduplicates results, bounds creator-history inspection, and avoids repeated channel requests where a current fact is not needed.

Storage and Retention

Trend Radar does not use raw YouTube API responses as durable product intelligence. Retention-bound YouTube identifiers, public display metadata, and factual observations are separated from safer derived analytical states. Under the current default policy, retention-bound metadata, statistical observations, and derived metrics are generally subject to 30-day retention deadlines; Trend Radar does not claim approval for extended retention.

Some derived analytical states may be retained separately where permitted and where they do not contain or reconstruct retention-bound YouTube facts. Those states remain subject to applicable policy and product controls.

Refresh and Deletion

Trend Radar evaluates retention-bound records against their deadlines. Current facts may be refreshed through the applicable API method when needed. Expired or unavailable facts are removed, withheld from normal product use, or represented without stale display data according to the active retention controls. Data may also be deleted when no longer needed or when required by applicable YouTube API Services policies.

Public Identifiers and Display Information

Public identifiers and display information may be used to associate evidence with the correct public video or creator and to provide links or names in the product while that information remains lifecycle-valid. Trend Radar does not treat a public channel identifier as private account access, and it does not expose internal retention or lifecycle identifiers to ordinary users.

Derived Analytics

Trend Radar creates analytical states from available public facts, including evidence coverage, recurrence, pattern, trend, and creator-opportunity states. These are Trend Radar's interpretations of bounded evidence, not additional YouTube facts. Factual qualification and derived analysis remain separate, and missing or incomplete evidence is not fabricated.

What Trend Radar Does Not Do

Trend Radar does not access private YouTube account data or request private-account authorization for its current public discovery product, collect user passwords, download YouTube videos or audio, acquire transcripts through media downloading, scrape YouTube webpages as part of its API workflow, or analyze video frames or thumbnails as factual input. It does not sell raw YouTube API data.

Third-Party Services

The public interface is hosted using Streamlit Community Cloud. Trend Radar relies on YouTube API Services for public YouTube metadata. When separately enabled, Google Gemini API may receive a minimized payload containing public video-title text, opaque evidence references and roles, and already-derived pattern labels, summaries, stable elements, and variable slots for pattern analysis. It is not used to determine factual YouTube eligibility. Trend Radar does not send creator names or public handles, channel or video identifiers, URLs, descriptions, raw API payloads, filesystem paths, secrets, or private data through that semantic workflow.

Third-party services may process technical request information under their own terms and privacy policies. Trend Radar does not control those services' independent practices.
