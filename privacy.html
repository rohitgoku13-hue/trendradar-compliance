Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Trend Radar, an independent analytics and research project operated by its individual developer, handles data used by the service.

Information Trend Radar uses

Trend Radar uses publicly available information returned by YouTube API Services for analytics and research. Depending on availability, this may include public video and channel identifiers, titles, descriptions, publication times, duration, official category information, tags, public view, like, and comment counts, channel names, and public subscriber-count observations.

Trend Radar does not collect user passwords. It currently does not require end-user Google sign-in and does not receive end-user OAuth data.

Purpose of use

The data is used to conduct deterministic public-data analytics, including factual filtering, provenance validation, and research-oriented evidence analysis. Derived metrics are generated independently by Trend Radar from the collected public metadata.

YouTube API Services

Trend Radar uses YouTube API Services, including YouTube Data API v3 endpoints such as search.list, videos.list, and channels.list.

Please review the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Data minimization, retention, refresh, and deletion

Trend Radar requests only public API data needed for its defined analytics workflow. It stores provenance-bound research artifacts so results can be verified and audited. The project does not claim approval for extended retention of YouTube API data.

The developer reviews stored API-derived information and will refresh or delete it as required by applicable YouTube API Services policies and operational needs. This policy does not promise a retention period that is inconsistent with those requirements.

What Trend Radar does not do

Trend Radar does not sell user personal information, scrape YouTube webpages, download YouTube videos, or acquire video, audio, frames, or transcripts as part of its public-metadata analytics workflow.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the public issue tracker of the Trend Radar compliance-site repository.
