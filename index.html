What Trend Radar does

Turns public-video evidence into an explorable radar.

Discover emerging patterns

Explore a consumer hierarchy from categories to dynamically discovered topics, trends or content niches, supporting videos, and creators.

Focus on smaller creators

Trend Radar applies factual eligibility and quality checks to public video and channel metadata. Evidence satisfying those checks may proceed to bounded creator-history analysis. Repeated creator performance contributes to trend intelligence; no single fact guarantees a result.

Keep coverage current

Bounded Search continues to find newly appearing creators and videos. Previously discovered qualifying creators may be checked directly for new public uploads, reducing unnecessary repeated Search.

How data is handled

Public metadata, not downloaded media.

Trend Radar uses current public metadata returned by YouTube API Services. It does not download YouTube video or audio, scrape YouTube webpages as part of its API workflow, acquire transcripts through media downloading, or analyze frames or thumbnails as factual input.

Retention-bound YouTube identifiers and display metadata are kept separate from safer derived analytical states and are subject to refresh and deletion controls. Trend Radar does not sell raw YouTube API data.
